# Player Guide — Vạn Thế Tu Tiên

## Getting Started

### 1. Create Your Character
- Use `/taonhanvat` to create your character
- Choose your name and start your cultivation journey

### 2. Basic Commands
- `/hoso` — View your profile and stats
- `/lamviec` — Work to earn Linh Thạch
- `/linhdien` — Check leyline status
- `/nhiemvu` — View daily quests

### 3. First Steps
1. Complete daily quests for steady progress
2. Join a sect for EXP bonuses
3. Train your first pet
4. Start exploring the world

## Game Mechanics

### Cultivation
- **Meditation**: Active cultivation for EXP
- **Idle Cultivation**: passive EXP while offline
- **Breakthrough**: Level up through major realms
- **Prestige**: Reset at max level for permanent bonuses

### Combat
- **Skills**: 6 elemental skills with mastery
- **Equipment**: Enhance and reforge items
- **Sets**: Equip matching items for bonuses
- **Pets**: Combat companions

### Social
- **Sects**: Join a guild for benefits
- **Marriage**: Partner with another player
- **Mentor**: Teach apprentices

### Economy
- **Market**: Trade with other players
- **Crafting**: Create items and equipment
- **Exploration**: Find treasures

## Strategy Guide

### Early Game (Level 1-50)
- Focus on daily quests and meditation
- Join a sect early
- Train your first pet
- Save Linh Thach for important upgrades

### Mid Game (Level 50-200)
- Start participating in PvP
- Complete dungeon runs
- Build equipment sets
- Level up your sect

### Late Game (Level 200-380)
- Focus on skill mastery
- Build optimal equipment
- Participate in tournaments
- Prepare for prestige

### Endgame (Prestige+)
- Reset for permanent bonuses
- Chase leaderboards
- Complete all achievements
- Master all systems

## Tips
1. Log in daily for streak bonuses
2. Join a sect early for EXP bonuses
3. Train pets for combat bonuses
4. Complete daily quests for steady progress
5. Save Linh Thach for important upgrades
6. Check market before crafting
7. Complete weekly quests for bonus rewards
8. Participate in events for exclusive items
