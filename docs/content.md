# Content Documentation — Vạn Thế Tu Tiên

## Items

### Equipment
- **Weapons**: Swords, axes, staves, etc.
- **Armor**: Chest, legs, gloves, boots
- **Accessories**: Rings, necklaces, belts

### Consumables
- **Pills**: HP, MP, EXP, breakthrough
- **Food**: Temporary stat buffs
- **Materials**: Crafting ingredients

### Special
- **Tinh Thach Shards**: Enhancement materials
- **Soul Essence**: Weapon evolution
- **Beast Food**: Pet training
- **Destiny Shards**: Destiny shop currency

## Skills

### Elemental Skills
- **Fire (Hỏa)**: Burn damage, AoE
- **Water (Thủy)**: Healing, cleanse
- **Earth (Thổ)**: Shield, taunt
- **Wind (Phong)**: Dodge, speed
- **Lightning (Lôi)**: Stun, burst damage
- **Wood (Mộc)**: Lifesteal, regen
- **Metal (Kim)**: Crit, armor pierce

### Mastery System
- Levels 1-10 per skill
- Passive bonuses at each level
- Skill paths at level 5
- Combos at level 3+

## Achievements

### Categories
- **Tu Luyện**: Cultivation milestones
- **Chiến Đấu**: Combat achievements
- **PvP**: Player vs Player
- **Sủng Thú**: Pet collection
- **Sinh Hoạt**: Life activities
- **Ẩn**: Hidden achievements

### Weekly Achievements
- 5 rotating achievements per week
- Bonus rewards for completion

### Seasonal Achievements
- 10 achievements per season
- Exclusive seasonal rewards

## Quests

### Daily Quests
- 3 quests per day
- Level scaling rewards
- Streak bonuses (7 days)

### Weekly Quests
- 5 quests per week
- Tier rewards (3/5, 4/5, 5/5)

### Quest Chains
- Main story chains
- Side story chains
- Guild chains
- Couple chains
- Mentor chains

## Events

### Dynamic Events
- World Boss spawns
- Leyline surges
- Thiên Kiếp events
- Seasonal events

### Rotation System
- Daily rotations (dungeon, boss, crafting, exploration, PvP)
- Weekly rotations (dungeon, boss, challenge, market, guild)
- Monthly seasons (theme, dungeon, boss, rewards)
